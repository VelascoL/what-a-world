About the collection

What a world, huh?

A shared field journal of encounters with the natural world: moments that made someone stop, look again, or feel something they hadn’t expected.

Some happen on a mountain. Some happen beside the kitchen sink. They can be joyful, unsettling, ordinary, or difficult to put into words.

A few words or a whole story.
Whatever comes naturally.

The collection has room for all of them.
