I was in a toxic relationship for almost 3 years. It got to the point where I was obsessed, entirely dependent, and my world and everything I thought of or acted on was for him. When he broke up with me, I made a plan to attempt suicide. 

The first weeks were awful; I couldn't eat or sleep or even move without it costing all of my energy. My mom started taking me on walks once I could stand it more or less. The first time she did, my plan to commit would've been right after. She took me to our local park around golden hour. After spending weeks alone in my room, after my only light being from my phone, after losing my shit, I looked up. I looked up at the sunset, and all the gorgeous colors in the sky. I looked at the flowers, and how the light bouncing off them made them glow a golden hue. I looked at the river, and how the highlights on the surface appeared as though they were dancing to the chirping of the birds. I reached down to touch it, and the cold sent shivers all over my body. It was the first time I felt anything other than numbness, exhaustion, depression, or rage. It felt like breathing. 

I think if I hadn't gone outside that day, I wouldn't be writing this. Of course I wasn't immediately better, but taking the first breath is usually a sign of life after being unconscious for so long.
