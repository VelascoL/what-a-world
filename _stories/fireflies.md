---
title: "The night the trees lit up"
contributor: "Fictional sample"
place: "A summer evening"
order: 1
sample: true
---

We turned off our flashlights. For a moment, there was nothing. Then the whole tree began to blink.

I had seen fireflies before, but never so many, and never so far above my head. They made the branches seem to appear and disappear, as if the tree were being drawn again every few seconds.

We stood there until we forgot we had been on our way somewhere.
